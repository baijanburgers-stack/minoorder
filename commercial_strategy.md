## 💎 Value Proposition for Restaurant Owners

MinoOrder is marketed to restaurant owners as an **All-in-One compliance-first Horeca operating system** that cuts terminal licensing costs, improves order throughput, and prevents tax auditing risks.

1.  **Reduce Order Latency by 40%**: The high-performance Self-Service Kiosks guide customers through quick categorical grids and visual combo step wizards.
2.  **Mitigate Tax Audit Penalties**: Built-in dynamic VAT extraction and proportional combo discount distribution algorithms ensure that every checkout receipt conforms perfectly to legal standards.
3.  **Uninterrupted Service (Offline First)**: With local outbox sync databases, cashier POS tablets keep routing payments and printing receipts even when the restaurant's internet drops.
4.  **Hardware Independence**: Built-in printer abstraction adapters allow store managers to plug in any Star, Epson, or Sunmi thermal hardware without driver conflicts.

## 📈 Go-To-Market & Store Acquisition Playbook

To acquire the first 10 pilot restaurant chains in Belgium:

### Phase 1: Local Pilot Store Acquisition (Weeks 1-4)
*   **Target Group**: Independent high-traffic bistros and waffle shops in Brussels, Ghent, and Antwerp.
*   **Offer**: 3-Month Free Trial of the **Growth Tier** including free local printer routing setup.
*   **Goal**: Gather operational logs, cashier feedback, and verify FDM hardware sync stability.

### Phase 2: Compliance-First Direct Marketing (Weeks 5-8)
*   **Marketing Angle**: *"Are your combo discounts tax-compliant?"*
*   **Strategy**: Educate restaurant managers on the legal audits surrounding combo VAT rounding errors in Belgium. Pitch MinoOrder's **Proportional Discount Allocation Engine** as an automated shield against tax fines.
*   **Collateral**: Simple landing pages on `minoorder.com` with a free interactive VAT combo calculator widget (powered by our Next.js backend code!).

### Phase 3: Franchise & Chain Expansion (Weeks 9-12)
*   **Target Group**: Multi-location Horeca franchises and chain managers.
*   **Offer**: Bulk licensing pricing (e.g. €199/month per store for 5+ locations).
*   **Pitch**: Centralized multi-tenant management: Super Admins can update menus, push menu snapshots, track real-time sales, and analyze shift audits across all locations instantly from a single Next.js Back Office portal.
